Rules

Attendance and assessment

  • Attend scheduled classes, labs, exams and required activities; attendance can affect exam access and progression.
  • Check your attendance regularly. If an entry looks wrong, note the course, date and evidence and contact the faculty or academic office.
  • Approved leave doesn't automatically remove an absence from attendance.
  • Assessment includes internal assessments, assignments, lab work and end-semester exams.
  • Submit original work, follow exam-room rules and never share your account or impersonate another student.

Certificates

Certificate collection

Collect the no-dues form from the Examination Cell and get each signature in this order.

  1. Examination CellCollect the certificate application / no-dues form and fill it in.
  2. Finance DepartmentClear outstanding dues and get the Finance signature.
  3. LibraryReturn books and any college laptop or equipment; get the Library signature.
  4. Department HODGet your HOD's signature.
  5. Hostel WardenComplete hostel clearance and get the warden's signature.
  6. Student Wellness HeadGet the Student Wellness clearance signature.
  7. DirectorGet the Director's signature on the completed form.
  8. Examination CellSubmit the signed form; certificates are processed and issued.
