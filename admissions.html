Admissions 2026

The six-year integrated programme: two years of PUC followed by four years of B.Tech. Eligibility, selection, reservation, intake and dates are set in each year's admission notification.

Step by step

Admission process

  1. Read the notificationCheck eligibility, intake year, category rules, dates and instructions.
  2. Apply onlineEnter the personal, contact and school details the portal asks for.
  3. Upload documentsClear copies in the specified format.
  4. Submit and payPay the ₹500 application fee. Keep the acknowledgement and receipt.
  5. Track statusCheck the portal status and messages.
  6. Attend verificationGo on the assigned date with your original documents.
  7. Confirm admissionComplete payment, documents and joining steps.
Checklist

Documents

  • Photograph and signature
  • Qualifying school marks memo or equivalent academic record
  • Date-of-birth proof
  • Study certificates or school records for the specified years
  • Category, income or residence certificates (reserved categories)
  • Application acknowledgement and payment receipt
  • Original documents for verification

FAQ

Common questions

Can I edit my application after submitting?

Check whether a correction window is open. If not, contact the Admissions Office with your application number. Don't submit a duplicate application unless told to.

I paid the application fee but it still shows pending.

Keep the receipt, transaction reference, date and amount. Check the bank or gateway status, then contact Admissions or the Finance Office if the portal hasn't updated.

My status hasn't changed for a while.

Check portal messages and notices. If the stated processing time has passed, write to Admissions with your application number and acknowledgement.

How long is the programme?

Six years: two years of PUC and four years of B.Tech.

Questions about your application?

Write to the Admissions Office with your application number.
