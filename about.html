About RGUKT Nuzvid

Rajiv Gandhi University of Knowledge Technologies, Nuzvid offers a six-year integrated engineering programme on a residential campus.

Overview

The integrated pathway

The programme is structured as two years of Pre-University Course (PUC) followed by four years of B.Tech. Students study, live and take part in campus life in one place, with hostels, a mess, a central library, laboratories, sports facilities and student wellness services.

Admission is through an annual notification that sets eligibility, selection, reservation, intake and dates.

Years 1–2 · PUC

Pre-University Course that prepares students for engineering study.

Years 3–6 · B.Tech

Four years in an engineering branch, with laboratories and project work.

Departments

Academic departments

CSE

Computer Science and Engineering

Programming, algorithms, databases, operating systems, networks and software engineering.

ECE

Electronics and Communication Engineering

Electronic circuits, communication systems, embedded systems and signals.

EEE

Electrical and Electronics Engineering

Electrical machines, power systems, control systems and energy systems.

MECH

Mechanical Engineering

Design, manufacturing, thermal systems, mechanics and materials.

CIVIL

Civil Engineering

Structures, construction materials, surveying and transportation.

CHEM

Chemical Engineering

Process engineering, chemical operations, thermodynamics and process control.

MME

Metallurgical and Materials Engineering

Metals, materials processing and characterisation.
