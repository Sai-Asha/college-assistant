Paying

How to pay

At the bank

Use the Director's account number and instructions printed on the college fee form. Submit the payment form and receipt to Finance if asked.

Online · SBI Collect

Select the institution and fee category, enter your details and save the final receipt.

Paid but still pending?

Keep the receipt and transaction reference, check the bank status, then contact the Finance Office. Don't pay again until it's checked.
