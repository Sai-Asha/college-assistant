About the college

Engineering education from the foundation up

Students join after school and spend their first two years in the Pre-University Course before moving into four years of B.Tech in their chosen branch. Teaching, laboratories, the central library, hostels and student services all sit on one campus.

Structures, construction materials, surveying and transportation.

CHEM
